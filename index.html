What is Neurofeedback?

"Teach the brain
to teach itself"

Neurofeedback is a drug-free, non-invasive therapy that adjusts inefficient brainwave patterns to improve symptoms associated with anxiety, depression, ADHD, PTSD, autism, insomnia, and more.

Using a QEEG brain map, we capture a detailed picture of your brain's electrical activity. Neurofeedback training then uses sound or visual signals to gently guide the brain toward healthier, more balanced patterns — producing long-term, lasting change.

Home Training

Train from the comfort of your home

For clients who need flexible scheduling or prefer training in their own environment, we offer clinician-supervised home neurofeedback programs. This allows you to train daily at times that work best for you, without the burden of traffic or appointment scheduling.

Using a lightweight EEG headset and a simple app, you'll complete personalized brain training sessions designed by Gina based on your QEEG results. You can train while watching videos, playing games, or streaming content — the system provides real-time feedback as your brain learns to self-regulate.

About

The interplay between your body and brain is paramount in healing

Functional Interplay Therapy is a privately owned practice founded by Angelina (Gina) Brehm OTR/L, BCN. Gina graduated with her Masters of Occupational Therapy from Xavier University and is board certified in Neurofeedback Therapy through BCIA.

In 2022, Gina transitioned her service model to emphasise Neurofeedback. She continues to use an occupational therapy lens to assess the occupational needs of her clients — offering a genuinely holistic approach to neurological health.

Through the combination of Neurofeedback and her Sensory Integration background, Gina works with both pediatric and adult clients to achieve greater regulation, improved cognitive function, and lasting quality of life improvements.
